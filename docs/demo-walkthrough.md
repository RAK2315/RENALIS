# RENALIS walkthrough · v0.3 experience

Start the API and Vite with `scripts/start.ps1`, then open http://127.0.0.1:5173/. All built-in people and acquired device values are synthetic. The default page is the product introduction. Click **Explore the guided demo** for a six-step orientation, or **Open workspace** for direct access. **Research & evidence** opens sources, architecture, live reports, validation, and project documents.

1. **Patient twin → Meera Rao → minute 90.** Observe the historical and device evidence, fluid accounting, and model forecast. Move to minute 120: the card now says **CURRENT OBSERVATION / Current low BP**, displays 75.7 mmHg, and explicitly identifies an existing event. It does not display a null percentage or a “next 30 minutes” heading. Return to minute 90.
2. **Mechanism lab.** If the experiment already has observations, click **Reset lab observations**. Start with 650 mL/h, constant profile, all channels available, and burden 0.5×. The three family cards fit essentially the same available BP history. Click each card to emphasize its future curve. The model-dependent responses differ, so the comparison remains unresolved.
3. **What evidence would help?** Immediate noninvasive cardiac output has the greatest net value in this setting. Inspect the candidate table: another immediate BP reading has almost zero decision value. Information gain in bits and loss reduction are separate columns. Noise SD, burden, timing, and the loss definition are inspectable.
4. **Acquire synthetic measurement.** The synthetic device releases 3.74 L/min at minute 90. Watch vascular support fall and volume/cardiac support increase. The comparison can remain unresolved: evidence can narrow an uncertainty without settling it. A previously acquired channel/time is not proposed again or counted twice.
5. **Save comparison → Original report.** The immutable report retains its measurement, support weights, assumptions, and revision. Reset observations; the live lab returns to its BP-only comparison, while the original saved report stays unchanged. **Restore** restores settings and explicitly recomputes against currently active evidence.
6. **Channel availability and burden.** Reset, then uncheck output and relative volume, leaving cuff only. No candidate clears the net-value threshold. Uncheck cuff too: no device is available and the result remains unresolved. Restore all channels. Increase burden to 3.0×: no observation is worth its declared cost. Return to 0.5×.
7. **Optional alternate observation.** Expand **Enter a research measurement manually**. With a reset experiment, select cardiac output and enter 5.1 L/min. The vascular model receives most compatibility support and the credible families agree under the stated loss. This is agreement inside the chosen models, not an identified diagnosis. Reset again.
8. **Try to break the assumptions.** Enter relative blood volume 120% manually. This is inside the accepted sandbox input range but outside every modeled family's plausible measurement range. Observe the contradiction audit, “Model assumptions no longer fit,” disabled scenario control, and withheld curves/measurement schedule. Reset archives the evidence and restores the lab.
9. **Inspect scenario support.** Compare factual BP fit, settings already observed in this session, mechanism dependence, and absent intervention evidence. Select points in the fluid-removal versus minimum-BP plot. A good factual fit does not make an extrapolated intervention credible. Adjust authored net interdialytic gain and inspect the explicit next-session accounting; it is not a predicted intake or prescribed fluid target.
10. **Run challenge suite.** Inspect 108 independent synthetic futures, the common one-observation budget, all three policy results, and safeguards. The decision-value policy does not beat fixed cuff on mean regret here. Download every scored case and inspect failures; a more informative observation does not guarantee a better decision under model mismatch.
11. **Guard states.** Meera at minute 120 withholds research comparisons because BP is already low. Leela at minute 90 withholds them because BP is stale. At minute 240 the session is complete and forecasts stop. The original Patient twin and Mechanism lab both distinguish these states from a prospective warning.

The original Patient twin, its synthetic benchmark, the new mechanism challenge suite, and the observed HEMOBP baseline are different experiments. No observed dataset has validated the mechanism identities or the treatment effects in this demonstration.
