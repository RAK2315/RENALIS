# RENALIS model card

Versions: original Patient twin `volume-particle-twin/0.1`; separate Mechanism lab `competing-mechanisms/0.3`. Local research prototype, updated 16 October 2026.

## Target and scope

Maintenance hemodialysis in chronic kidney disease. Forecast a new measured systolic BP ≤90 mmHg within a 30-minute horizon. Current low BP is labeled as an existing event, not an advance alert. The horizon is shortened at session end; completed sessions produce no additional treatment forecast.

## Sandbox state estimator

Historical synthetic EHR priors initialize reference circulating volume, excess tissue volume, BP baseline, refill capacity and compensation. New available BP measurements update a 192-particle posterior through a robust observation likelihood and particle resampling. Device removal observations drive a conservative fluid model at one-minute steps. The estimator uses no future BP, later device changes or hidden simulated state.

Model-particle percentiles represent uncertainty conditional on the chosen equations and priors. They do not account for every clinical source of uncertainty and are not clinically calibrated intervals. The fraction crossing a threshold is not a calibrated real-world probability.

## Sandbox safeguards

BP observations marked artifact are excluded. Event alerting and scenario ranking are withheld when the last valid BP is older than 15 minutes. Already-low BP is identified separately. Scenario branches share the same posterior starting state and expose remaining fluid consequences. The UI records local review notes and simulation lineage only; it sends no treatment commands.

The sandbox threshold, observation cadence, stale-data policy and parameter distributions are research choices. They require clinical review before any real deployment.

## Synthetic benchmark

Computed persistence, history/machine, population-prior/dynamic and personalized-fused forecasts over a fixed set of four fictional patients. Overlapping windows are not independent clinical events. Shared model structure between generator and estimator can inflate apparent performance. These results verify the pipeline within a sandbox; they do not establish clinical accuracy.

## Separate HEMOBP observed baseline

The training script audits actual field names, collapses carried-forward BP packets by measurement identifier, infers episodes from time resets/gaps, and joins patient demographics. Next distinct BP targets are restricted to 5–35 minute gaps and currently non-hypotensive readings. Regression baselines use patient-held-out test data. A low-BP event classifier uses a separate patient-disjoint validation subset for threshold selection.

Source ultrafiltration units and episode identities remain audit limitations. Post-session weight and future machine settings are excluded. The observed baselines are not yet assimilated into the synthetic physiological twin. Benchmark outputs, sample sizes, model artifacts and limitations are generated by the script rather than copied from a paper.

## Competing mechanisms and measurement value

The Mechanism lab fits three explicitly authored volume/refill, vascular, and cardiac response families to the same available history, with 48 particles per family. Local pressure factors permit similar factual BP fits with different hypothetical response curves. The family weights are conditional compatibility within this chosen model set, not posterior clinical diagnosis probabilities. The model set need not contain a patient's actual physiology.

Immediate or permissible delayed cuff BP, relative blood volume, and cardiac output observations are evaluated using Gaussian quadrature over possible measurements. Expected minimum scenario-loss reduction is separate from family information gain. The loss, measurement noise, burden multiplier, and delay penalty are exposed in the UI and exported report. Delayed actions retain current removal until the measurement time. Measurement selection cannot access the synthetic acquisition fixture or future observations. No available or sufficiently valuable channel produces an unresolved/no-observation state.

Noise SD assumptions are 3.5 mmHg, 1.0 relative-volume percentage point, and 0.30 L/min. They are research choices, not specifications of clinical devices. The cardiac-output and relative-volume observation maps are proxies requiring matched physiological data for calibration. Synthetic acquisition is deterministic and isolated from inference. Manual values are range-validated research observations. Only observations whose availability minute is at or before the cutoff are assimilated, and repeated channel/time acquisitions are idempotent.

Any acquired observation more than four assumed noise SD away from every particle, or a minimum family factual-fit RMSE over 8 mmHg, downgrades authority. This is a simple inconsistency check, not validated drift detection. The lab withholds scenario forecasts, ranked preferences, observation experiments, and carry-over projections for contradiction, stale BP, already-low BP, or session completion. Reset archives evidence; immutable saved reports retain original evidence and revision. Automatic clinical recovery/refitting has not been implemented.

The support landscape separates factual fit, proximity to this session's observed settings (a ±100 mL/h heuristic), mechanism dependence, and absent causal identification. It is not a safety map or observational propensity estimate. Carry-over adds modeled unfinished fluid target to user-authored net gain; it is explicit accounting, not a validated longitudinal twin.

## Independent synthetic observation-policy challenge

The reproducible suite uses 108 additive-response cases with scoring futures independent of the twin's compartment pressure equations. Three policies share an at-most-one-observation budget and the same pre-generated values for any chosen channel. Mean independent regret is 0.2655 (fixed cuff), 0.2290 (family information), and 0.2656 (decision value minus burden). Eight cases under each adaptive policy are worse than the cuff comparison. Decision-value policy superiority is not demonstrated. Authored mechanism/sensor distributions, shared declared loss, and synthetic cases limit interpretation. The suite does not assess delayed measurements or prospective clinical outcomes. All scored cases are retained in `docs/originality-challenges.json`.

## Intended use and exclusions

Intended for hackathon research demonstration, physiological-model development and retrospective evaluation. Not for diagnosis, real-time treatment recommendations, device control or claims of reducing admissions/mortality. No Indian population generalization or causal effect of changing removal profiles has been demonstrated.

## Next scientific work

Independently reconcile HEMOBP sessions and units; evaluate scheduled versus clinically prompted readings; consolidate alerts at event/patient-day level; calibrate prospective scores and intervals on separate patients; address the observed policy failures without tuning against a purported held-out suite; fit identifiable physiological parameters using matched observed data; and obtain clinical review of endpoint and sandbox assumptions.
